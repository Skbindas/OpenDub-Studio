## Steps
1. **Hardware and capacity plan** — extend `backend/system_diagnostics.py`, add a resource planner and project capacity endpoint. Detect physical/logical CPU cores, available RAM, dedicated GPU/VRAM and usable engine runtimes separately. Estimate working storage from video duration, retain OS headroom and cap workers/threads conservatively. Do not promise a GPU speedup from hardware detection alone.
   - Verify low-RAM, CPU-only, large-GPU and low-disk fixtures; inspect the actual laptop plan in diagnostics.
2. **Live work progress and ETA** — report actual analysis checkpoint, ASR processed seconds, completed/remaining synthesis and fit units. Save progress durably, include worker messages and derive elapsed/ETA from measured work. Show “calibrating” until enough work has finished; retain progress on browser/network reconnect.
   - Verify percentages advance within stages, retries do not restart overall progress, ETA remains unknown when there is no measured rate, and UI progress matches backend work.
3. **Durable chunk execution** — deterministic chunk IDs, input/model fingerprints, atomic status files, bounded workers and per-worker logs. Validate outputs before marking completion; reuse only validated chunks. On interruption, unfinished chunks become resumable while completed outputs remain intact.
   - Verify injected failure after one completed chunk, coordinator restart and recovery, out-of-order completion, stale/missing output rejection and disk admission.
4. **Long-video media integration** — bounded audio extraction/transcription/separation with timestamp offsets and contextual boundaries; stream stem assembly in original order. Keep global speaker identity and full timeline intact, preserve original video, and use RF64 or chunked intermediate formats beyond the WAV 4 GB limit. Cap GPU concurrency per usable device; use isolated CPU helpers when CUDA is unavailable. Helper terminals may be enabled on Windows as requested; main terminal remains open.
   - Verify short sample before feature-length trials, speech across chunk boundaries, no missing/duplicated audio frames, original BGM/SFX levels in the final mix and memory/storage admission for 2–5-hour durations. Production parallel mode is enabled only after these checks pass.
5. **Review/retry controls and diagnostics** — keep stage retry buttons, distinguish timing review from infrastructure failure, show chosen worker/device plan and actionable dependency warnings. On internet loss, continue cached local engines; missing model downloads wait/fail resumably instead of resetting work.
   - Verify in the Codex in-app browser only. Exercise reload, disconnect/reconnect and retry. Do not invoke deletion controls during testing.
6. **Delivery** — focused regressions, one full suite for integrated changes, sample end-to-end checks, clean commit/push and monitoring of every triggered GitHub workflow.

## Risks and safeguards
Multiple terminals do not themselves make work faster; oversubscribed models can exhaust RAM/VRAM. Bounded process admission and reserved capacity govern concurrency. Chunked speaker clustering needs cross-chunk identity validation; separately detected speaker numbers cannot be blindly merged. Estimated separator stems are not certified lossless. Timing review must never be bypassed by truncating dialogue or aggressive stretching. Long-video support is not certified merely by planning a five-hour duration.
